How scores work

The score is a research triage tool. It is not a forecast, price target, or recommendation.

QUALITY · 0–100

Higher is stronger

  • Financial resilience · 35%
  • Business durability · 25%
  • Governance & disclosure · 20%
  • Deal structure · 20%
RISK · 0–100

Higher means more risk

  • Valuation risk · 30%
  • Execution/profitability · 25%
  • Market/sector · 15%
  • Liquidity/deal · 15%
  • Country/regulatory · 15%
Research score = 0.55 × Quality + 0.45 × (100 − Risk)

Missing information

Scores require at least 60% data completeness. Below that threshold, the tracker shows “Insufficient data” instead of silently assigning neutral values. Scores marked provisional have less than 80% completeness.

Data policy

Primary sources take priority: regulator filings, prospectuses, exchange notices and issuer announcements. Dates are labelled confirmed, expected or unknown. Source conflicts are shown rather than resolved silently.
